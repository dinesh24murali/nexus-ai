# Research Brief: Grid-Scale Battery Storage 2026

## Market

Global grid-scale battery capacity reached 190 gigawatt-hours installed by the end of 2025 and is forecast to reach 410 gigawatt-hours by 2028.

## Costs

Lithium iron phosphate (LFP) cell prices fell to 52 dollars per kilowatt-hour in 2025, which is 40 percent lower than in 2023.

## Leading markets

China holds 55 percent of installed capacity, the United States 20 percent and Europe 12 percent.

## Technology

LFP dominates new installations with about 85 percent share. Sodium-ion batteries entered pilot production in 2025 at roughly 70 dollars per kilowatt-hour. They carry a lower fire risk and need no lithium or cobalt.

## Risks

Grid connection queues in Europe average 4 years. Fire safety rules for large sites are tightening. Recycling capacity covers less than 10 percent of end-of-life batteries.
